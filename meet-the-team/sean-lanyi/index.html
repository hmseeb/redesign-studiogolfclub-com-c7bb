Sean Lanyi

Putting Specialist

Location: South Bay, CA

Email

Sean is a Class “A” PGA Golf Professional, Level III AimPoint Express, SAM Puttlab and Putt Doctor Certified Instructor. Lanyi was recognized as a Golf Digest Best Young Teachers and Golf Digest Best in State. Sean was recognized as a Golf Range Association of America Top 100 Teaching Professional in America and U.S. Kids Golf Master Kids Teacher. Lanyi takes into consideration the player’s unique goals, what they are capable of physically achieving by extensive training and understanding of how their body works. By having a glass half full perspective and zest for life as a coach, Lanyi builds friendships along your journey towards playing better golf. Keep this in mind, if a golfer can understand ones tendencies, they’ll learn how to manage their game better under pressure! I look forward to being your coach and seeing you become a better golfer!
