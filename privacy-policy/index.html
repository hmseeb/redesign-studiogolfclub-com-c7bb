Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8/4/25

Studio Golf Club (“we,” “us,” or “our”) values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit studiogolfclub.com.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you interact with our website, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location
  • Any other details submitted via contact forms or service inquiries

Booking & Payment Information

When you schedule an appointment or make a payment, your data is securely handled by our third-party provider, Mindbody. We do not collect or store sensitive payment data directly on our servers. Mindbody may collect:

  • Appointment details (service booked, time, location)
  • Payment information
  • Customer account information

To learn more, please refer to the Mindbody Privacy Policy.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our site, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referring site or source
  • Cookies and tracking technologies

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries or service requests
  • Allow you to book lessons, fitness sessions, or fittings
  • Communicate important updates or marketing emails (you can opt out anytime)
  • Improve website functionality and performance
  • Understand user behavior via analytics

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, pixels, and analytics tools like:

  • Google Analytics
  • Meta/Facebook Pixel

These tools help us understand website traffic and improve your experience. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Services

We may share or transmit data through trusted service providers that help us operate our business, including:

  • Mindbody for scheduling and payments
  • Email marketing providers like Mailchimp
  • Analytics platforms like Google and Meta

These providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

5. How We Protect Your Information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure. If a data breach were ever to occur, we would take prompt action to notify users and comply with applicable laws.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You can:

  • Request access, correction, or deletion of your data
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails
  • Opt out of non-essential cookies or analytics tracking
  • Contact us for any privacy-related inquiries

Reach out anytime at sminke@studiogolfclub.com.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our site and services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may revise this policy as our services evolve. Any updates will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
