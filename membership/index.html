What's included

Membership means all access

We’re not a country club. We’re not a big-box gym. We’re something better.

01

Train Without Limits

Hit when you want, how you want. Unlimited bay time and access to the tools, tech, and space you need to sharpen your game without watching the clock.

02

Bring Your People

Whether it’s your coach, your training partner, or your weekend crew, members can bring guests to share the space and the session.

03

Full Club Access

That includes coaching, recovery, strength and mobility training, custom fittings, and the best tech in the game, TrackMan, PuttView, and more.

04

Get Member Rates

Save on everything from private lessons to merch and fitting. Members get preferred pricing across the board.

05

Events, Only for Members

Compete in private leagues. Join pop-up clinics. Tap into a community of players who are all here to get better together.

06

Unlimited Practice Time

Members can book up to 2 hours of bay time every single day. The gym, wellness area, and putting green are always open to you.
