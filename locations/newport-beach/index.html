FAQ's

Newport Beach FAQs

When will Studio Golf Newport Beach open?

We will be opening in the fall of 2026. Founding members will receive regular construction updates, early access announcements, and invitations to all pre-opening events.

Can I bring guests?

Yes! Founding members receive guest privileges and can bring unlimited friends, clients, or family members to enjoy the club experience with them as long as you are present with your guest.

Do I need to reserve a bay or can I walk in?

Members can reserve bays in advance through our booking app — available on iOS and Android.

As a member is there a limit to the number of bay hours I can use per month?

No, as a Studio Golf Member you can book up to 2 hours every single day.

Do I need to book the gym, the wellness area, or the putting green?

No, everything else is open and available for all members to use at any time.

Do I pay now or when you open?

To get the founding member benefits you will pay your first month or year upfront. The month or year you paid for will start on the first day we open.

Do I have access to the other Studio Golf locations?

Yes, once you are a founding member you can use any other Studio Golf location before and after we open.

If I bring a family member can they use other areas in the facility while I am in the bay?

Yes, for example if your wife wanted to sauna and cold plunge while you played a quick nine that is totally fine.

Is the monthly membership month-to-month even though I am locking in the lowest rate for a year?

Yes, although you are not committed to membership for a year, the founding membership pricing will remain for a full year.

Will you have showers and towel service?

Yes, we will have two private showers, a changing room and an open shower for rinsing. Towels will be provided at all times.

Are your saunas infrared or traditional?

We will have both! Additionally two of them will be private and one will be larger for a community feel.

Am I allowed to work at Studio Golf?

Yes, we encourage it. Our Members Bar and Lounge will have co-working spaces and desks to get your work done in style.
