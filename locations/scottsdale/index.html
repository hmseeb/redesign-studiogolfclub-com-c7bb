Club fitting

Gear that's built for your game

Most fittings stop at brand and shaft, but ours go deeper. At Studio Golf Scottsdale, we start by analyzing your full swing with TrackMan and Sportsbox AI to understand how you move and strike the ball. FAQ's

Studio Golf Scottsdale FAQs

How do I become a member of Studio Golf Scottsdale?

Becoming a member is easy. Simply visit the Scottsdale membership page and select the option that matches your goals. All members receive unlimited access to the club, preferred coaching rates, and full use of training, technology, and fitness facilities.

Can I book lessons or club fittings at Studio Golf Scottsdale if I’m not a member?

Yes. Non-members can book private coaching sessions, fitness training, or club fittings with our certified pros. A membership gives you preferred rates on all lessons, trainings, and fittings.

Can I reserve a bay at Studio Golf Scottsdale if I'm not a member?

No. Practice bays are reserved for members only. However, you can still book coaching sessions, fitness training, or club fittings with our certified pros—no membership required.

How much does a club fitting session cost at Studio Golf Scottsdale?

The cost depends on the type of fitting you choose. Static fittings start at $100, while dynamic fittings start at $200.
