FAQ's

Studio Golf Clearwater FAQs

How do I become a member of Studio Golf Clearwater?

Becoming a member is easy. Simply visit the Clearwater membership page and select the option that matches your goals. All members receive unlimited access to the club, preferred coaching rates, and full use of training, technology, and fitness facilities.

Can I book lessons or club fittings at Studio Golf Clearwater if I’m not a member?

Yes. Non-members can book private coaching sessions, fitness training, or club fittings with our certified pros. A membership gives you preferred rates on all lessons, trainings, and fittings.

Can I reserve a bay at Studio Golf Clearwater if I'm not a member?

No. Practice bays are reserved for members only. However, you can still book coaching sessions, fitness training, or club fittings with our certified pros—no membership required.

How much does a club fitting session cost at Studio Golf Clearwater?

The cost depends on the type of fitting you choose. Static fittings start at $100, while dynamic fittings start at $200.
