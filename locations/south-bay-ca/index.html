Club Fitting

At Studio Golf South Bay, club fitting is more than just picking a brand or shaft. It’s about matching your equipment to your swing with precision. We start by analyzing your swing data using TrackMan to measure ball speed, spin rates, launch angles, and attack path. Then we test a range of heads, shafts, and specs against real performance metrics to fine-tune feel, consistency, and distance.

FAQ's

Studio Golf South Bay FAQs

How do I become a member of Studio Golf South Bay?

Becoming a member is easy. Simply visit the South Bay membership page and select the option that matches your goals. All members receive unlimited access to the club, preferred coaching rates, and full use of training, technology, and fitness facilities.

Can I book lessons or club fittings at Studio Golf South Bay if I’m not a member?

Yes. Non-members can book private coaching sessions, fitness training, or club fittings with our certified pros. A membership gives you preferred rates on all lessons, trainings, and fittings.

Can I reserve a bay at Studio Golf South Bay if I'm not a member?

No. Practice bays are reserved for members only. However, you can still book coaching sessions, fitness training, or club fittings with our certified pros—no membership required.

How much does a club fitting session cost at Studio Golf South Bay?

The cost depends on the type of fitting you choose. Static fittings start at $100, while dynamic fittings start at $200.
