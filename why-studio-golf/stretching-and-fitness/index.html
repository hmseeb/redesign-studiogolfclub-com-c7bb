Personal Fitness

Strengthen your bodyGolf focused fitness.

Golf performance starts with your body. If you’re not moving well, you’re leaving distance and consistency on the table. Our expert-led sessions target strength, mobility, and flexibility to add power where you need it, protect you from injury, and keep you feeling fresh every round.

Pro fitness sessions

60 minutes of training. 30 minutes of recovery.

Every session blends 60 minutes of focused fitness with 30 minutes of guided stretch recovery, using OxeFit Smart Gym technology to measure power, velocity, load, and balance in real time. You’ll get detailed feedback on your movement, a customized plan to improve strength and mobility, and the tools to prevent injury and move better on the course.

  • Access to OxeFit Smart Gym
  • Personalized OxeAI training
  • 7-day fitness and stretching plan from your coach

OxeFit Smart Gym

Training informed by your body

This isn’t a row of treadmills or a corner of free weights. OxeFit uses robotics and real-time data to track every rep, every range of motion, and every detail of your form, including power, velocity, load, and balance. You’ll see your progress in real time, improve your form, and reduce the risk of injury. It’s tech-driven training that makes traditional gyms feel outdated.

Fitness Mobility Assessment

Know your starting point

Jumpstart your game with a 30-minute focused assessment designed to identify your mobility, flexibility, and movement limitations. Our team will pinpoint exactly where your body can improve, align your fitness goals with your golf game, and builds a plan to help you move better and perform stronger.

FAQ's

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be a member to book a Fitness & Stretch session?

No. Fitness & Stretch sessions are available to both members and non-members. Members, however, get exclusive access to the fitness area for independent training outside of scheduled sessions.

What’s included in a Fitness & Stretch session?

Each session combines 60 minutes of targeted fitness training with 30 minutes of stretch recovery, plus a 7-day personalized plan designed to keep you progressing between sessions.

How long are the sessions?

Sessions last 90 minutes total. (60 minutes of fitness followed by 30 minutes of stretch recovery)

Who designs the fitness programs?

Our programs are developed by trainers vetted and mentored by Don Saladino, world-renowned golf and celebrity fitness coach (Ryan Reynolds, Hugh Jackman, Jake Gyllenhaal). Don is the first-ever TPI Level 3 Fitness Certified Coach, mentored by TPI founder Dr. Greg Rose, and the founder of Golf 495, NYC’s first golf-specific gym. He’s been featured in Muscle & Fitness, Men’s Health, The Today Show, and serves as an NBC Golf Pass Fitness Coach and Trainer.

What is the Fitness Mobility Assessment?

The Fitness Mobility Assessment is a 30-minute evaluation that identifies your mobility, flexibility, and movement patterns. You’ll leave with a clear understanding of your strengths, limitations, and how to improve them.

Is the Fitness Mobility Assessment required before I start?

While not mandatory, we recommend starting with the assessment to get a baseline understanding of your body and to create a customized plan that matches your goals.
