Trackman

Ball & club analysis

TrackMan is a core part of every Studio Golf lesson. It gives you instant feedback on ball speed, spin rate, launch angle, attack angle, club path, face orientation, and more. This is how we identify root issues, confirm changes are working, and help you build a swing that holds up under pressure. It’s how we ensure every shot you hit at Studio Golf comes with an actionable takeaway.

SportsBox AI

3D swing analysis

Sportsbox AI uses a single video from your lesson to generate a complete 3D model of your swing without any sensors or extra gear. We use it to analyze movement joint by joint, measure rotation and sway, and show precisely how your mechanics are impacting your performance. This measurable data helps us understand your swing and show you how to fix it.

Touchscreen drills

Personalized practice on demand

After each session, your coach will upload a curated set of drills to our in-bay touchscreen system, so that when you come in to practice, you can follow a step-by-step plan built just for you. Watch demo videos, see your own swing comparisons, and stay locked in on the changes that move your game forward.

FAQ's

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I become a member of Studio Golf?

Becoming a member is easy. Simply visit the Membership page and select the option that best suits your goals. Members receive unlimited access to the club, preferred coaching rates, and full use of training, technology, and fitness facilities.

Can I book lessons if I’m not a member?

Yes. Non-members can book private lessons with any of our certified coaches. Every session includes swing analysis using TrackMan and Sportsbox AI, real-time feedback, and a personalized practice plan so you can keep improving after your session.

What does a lesson include?

Every session is one hour long and laser-focused on a specific area of improvement. You’ll get:

  • Club + ball flight data via TrackMan
  • 3D biomechanical breakdown via Sportsbox AI
  • Voiceover swing analysis
  • Drills and a takeaway improvement plan
  • Optional single sessions or discounted lesson packs
What’s the New Student Assessment?

It’s where every new golfer starts, an evaluation session designed to understand your swing, goals, and challenges. From there, we create a custom game plan tailored to you. You don’t need to prep or bring anything special—just show up ready to train.

What’s the difference between members and non-members?

Members have unrestricted training access. That means unlimited bay time, access to all fitness equipment, preferred booking times, and member pricing on lessons, club fittings, and events. Non-members are welcome to book individual sessions, but can’t use the facility independently.

How often should I take lessons?

It depends on your goals. Some members meet weekly, others space out sessions and train in between using their progress plans. Your coach will help you set the right cadence based on your current level and what you want to achieve.

Is this only for low-handicap golfers?

Nope. Whether you’re breaking 100 or chasing scratch, our coaching system adapts to your level. We’ve helped total beginners build solid foundations and competitive players take their game to the next level.
