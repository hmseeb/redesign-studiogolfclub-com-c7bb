Beyond the range

Train with purpose, not guesswork.

Every practice bay at Studio Golf is kitted out with TrackMan, Sportsbox AI, and PuttView. These are the same exact tools trusted by tour players. Reserve time when you need it. Train with purpose, not guesswork. Add a climate-controlled environment and a vibe built for focus, and you’ve got the best practice experience in golf.

Precision camera system

Three HD cameras, every angle

If Trackman and Sportsbox weren't enough, each bay is equipped with three high-definition cameras. One behind you and one on each side. They capture every angle of your swing, no matter your stance or dexterity. Forget propping up your iPhone, our cameras deliver instant replays and crystal-clear feedback that helps you fine-tune every move. This is precision tech designed to give you insights you simply can’t get anywhere else.

Touchscreen display

Personalized practice on demand

Each bay is equipped with an interactive touchscreen display loaded with a library of pre-recorded drills to sharpen your swing. Take the DIY route and choose the drills that align with your goals—or get a curated list of exercises from any coach after your first lesson. Every time you step into the bay, it’s like having a coach by your side, guiding you through the same structured, purposeful practice the pros rely on.

Trackman

Ball & club analysis

TrackMan isn’t just part of the bay, it powers it. Every swing you take runs through the same tour-level engine trusted by the best players in the world. From ball speed and spin rate to launch angle, attack angle, and club path, TrackMan delivers the precision data that makes our bays more than just a place to hit balls.

SportsBox AI

3D swing analysis

Sportsbox AI gives you access to a level of swing detail that’s almost impossible to find anywhere else. Using cutting-edge 3D motion capture and kinematic measurements, Sportsbox turns a single video into a complete 3D model of your swing—breaking down every move with pinpoint accuracy. Backed by industry leaders like Bryson DeChambeau, David Leadbetter, and Michelle Wie, this tech lets you see your mechanics in a way that’s both hyper-detailed and game-changing.

FAQ's

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be a member to reserve a practice bay?

Yes, practice bays are exclusive to Studio Golf members. Non-members can still book lessons, fitness sessions, and club fittings, but independent bay use is a member benefit.

How long can I reserve a practice bay?

Bays can be reserved for 1 or 2-hour sessions, with no limit on how often you book. Members have the freedom to train as much as they want, whenever they want.

What’s included when I book a practice bay?

You get full use of TrackMan, Sportsbox AI 3D analysis, and PuttView putting technology, plus our three-angle camera system with instant replay. Everything you need for focused, data-driven practice.

Can I bring guests inside the practice bay?

Yes. Members can bring guests to share their bay sessions as often as they’d like. However, the same guest can visit up to 6 times per year before they’ll need their own membership. It’s the perfect way to train with a friend or give someone a taste of the Studio Golf experience.

What if I just want to practice casually?

Even if you’re not chasing low scores, the bays make practice fun and productive. You can run TrackMan games, play a round or 2 on our available courses, or simply work on consistency with pro-level tools.
