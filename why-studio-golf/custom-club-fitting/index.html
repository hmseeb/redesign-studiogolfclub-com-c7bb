Custom Club Fitting

Built for your swing.Custom fit clubs that work with you.

Most golfers don’t realize they’re playing with clubs that don’t actually fit their game. A Studio Golf fitting isn’t about chasing the newest club, it’s about finding what truly works for your swing and your goals. And you don’t have to be a scratch player to benefit, dialing in your equipment is one of the fastest ways to improve your consistency and shave strokes off your handicap.

Our gear experts

Fitting players to the right gear is a craft

Fitting players to the right gear isn’t guesswork, it’s a craft. Our experts know every detail that goes into building the perfect set, from loft and lie to shaft profile and grip. Backed by tour-level data from TrackMan and years of hands-on experience, they’ll analyze your swing, test multiple setups, and fine-tune your equipment until every club is precisely built for your swing.

Benefits of club fitting

The right equipment unlocks performance you didn’t know you had.

With off-the-rack clubs, you’re working against the equipment, making the game harder than it needs to be. A proper fitting eliminates that struggle, giving you clubs built to work with your swing.

  1. Data-Driven Analysis

    Ball & Club. Using TrackMan, we capture every key metric, ball speed, launch angle, spin rates, and more. This takes the guesswork out of what's actually working for you. We then assess the performance of your current clubs, and where adjustments can be made.

  2. Club Testing & Adjustment

    Find the Right Gear. Most people think a fitting means hitting a few pre-built clubs and picking the best one. At Studio Golf, it’s far more precise. We test a variety of heads to find the one that feels and performs best for your swing, then pair it with multiple shaft options to dial in speed and dispersion. The result is a single club—built from the ground up, that’s perfectly matched to you.

  3. Fitting Report

    Detailed Recommendation for Your Clubs. Once we lock in the ideal combination of head, shaft, and grip, your fitter walks you through every detail, from why it works to how it performs. You will be provided a complete fitting report with your specs, recommended equipment, and shot data for full transparency.

FAQ's

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be a member to book a club fitting?

No. Club fittings are open to both members and non-members.

How much does a club fitting cost?

Pricing starts at $100 for static fittings and $200 for dynamic fittings, depending on the level of analysis.

How long does a fitting take?

Most fittings take 1–2 hours, depending on whether we’re fitting a single club or a full set.

Do I have to buy clubs after my fitting?

No. You’ll leave with a detailed fitting report, including your specs, recommendations, and launch data, so you can make your purchase when you’re ready.

Is club fitting only for advanced players?

Not at all! Club fitting benefits golfers of every skill level. In fact, for newer or mid-handicap players, a proper fitting is often the fastest way to improve consistency and drop strokes.

What’s included in a fitting?

You’ll test different club heads and shafts, with every combination analyzed using TrackMan launch data. We fine-tune loft, lie, and grip to build the perfect setup for your swing.

Do you fit all major brands?

Yes. We carry a wide range of top manufacturers to ensure we find the perfect equipment match for your game.
